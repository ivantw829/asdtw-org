ABOUT

About & Joining

Formosa Autistic Commune is a community hosted on Discord (primarily in Traditional Chinese). Please read this page before joining.

About Us

  • Founded: July 31, 2026.
  • Founder: Yi-Fan Lai. Personal website (opens in a new tab)
  • Size: over 440 members (November 2026).
  • Positions: We support pluralistic and progressive values. See our four core principles on the home page and our Positions page.
  • Actions: We organize members to participate in offline social movements; see Actions.

Who We Welcome

  • Disabled people of all support needs and disabilities.
  • People of any gender and any sexual orientation.

Non-disabled and heterosexual people are welcome to join, but this community prioritizes the needs of directly affected members. Please understand and respect who this space is centered on before joining.

Roles: Self-Selected, Self-Determined

After joining, you can pick your own roles under Discord’s “Channels & Roles” page. Selected roles appear on your Discord server profile. Multiple selections are allowed in every section—simply choose everything that applies to you.

  • Neurodiversity: Based on self-identification; no formal diagnosis is required. Options include autism, ADHD, bipolar, Tourette’s, OCD, dyslexia/learning disabilities, and selective mutism.
  • Sexual orientation: Pansexual, asexual, bisexual, homosexual, abrosexual, and heterosexual.
  • Taiwanese political party affinity: Optional, based on your own political leaning.
  • Event roles: For offline actions (such as Pride marches), react with the designated emoji under the announcement message to receive an event role for coordination.

Roles are visible on your server profile. Please decide which roles to select based on your own comfort and safety considerations.

Community Order

Administrators maintain order in accordance with the community’s charter. When a member’s words or actions prevent other members—especially disabled people and gender/sexual minorities—from participating safely, administrators will intervene and may remove the member from the community if necessary.

How to Join

  1. Prepare a Discord account.
  2. Follow the public invite link below, sign in, and accept the invitation.

Join Discord Community (opens in a new tab)

The invite link opens Discord’s website, where Discord’s own privacy policy and terms of service apply.
