ABOUT

About & Joining

Formosa Autistic Commune is a community hosted on Discord (primarily in Traditional Chinese). Please read this page before joining.

About Us

  • Founded: July 31, 2026.
  • Founder: Yi-Fan Lai. Personal website (opens in a new tab)
  • Size: over 440 members (November 2026).
  • Positions: We support diverse and progressive values. See our four core principles on the home page and our Positions page.
  • Actions: We have organized members to take part in social movements on the ground; see Actions.

Who We Welcome

  • Disabled people with any degree of disability.
  • People of any gender and any sexual orientation.

Non-disabled and heterosexual people are welcome to join, but this community prioritizes the needs of directly affected members. Please understand and respect who this space is centered on before joining.

Roles: Your Choice, Your Call

After joining, you can pick your own roles under Discord’s “Channels & Roles” page. Selected roles appear on your Discord server profile. Multiple selections are allowed in every section—simply choose everything that applies to you.

  • Neurodiversity: Based on self-identification; no formal diagnosis is required. Options include autism, ADHD, bipolar, Tourette’s, OCD, dyslexia/learning disabilities, and selective mutism.
  • Sexual orientation: Pansexual, asexual, bisexual, gay/lesbian, abrosexual, and heterosexual.
  • Taiwanese political party affinity: Choose according to your own political leaning.
  • Event roles: For each offline event (such as a march), react with the matching emoji under the announcement to receive a role used to coordinate the event.

Because roles appear on your profile, please consider your own safety when deciding which ones to take.

Community Order

Administrators maintain order in accordance with the community’s charter. When a member’s words or actions prevent other members—especially disabled and LGBTQ+ people—from feeling safe to participate, administrators will intervene and may remove the member from the community if necessary.

How to Join

  1. Have a Discord account ready.
  2. Follow the public invite link below, sign in, and accept the invitation.

Join Our Discord (opens in a new tab)

The invite link opens Discord’s website, where Discord’s own privacy policy and terms of service apply.
