Accessibility & Contact

We want everyone to be able to read this website comfortably. This page describes what we have done, what remains to be improved, and how to reach us.

How to Use Reading Settings

Every page has a “Reading settings” bar at the top, which requires JavaScript. Without JavaScript, the site remains fully readable and navigable in its default styling—including the language switcher—and works with your browser’s built-in zoom and high-contrast modes.

  • Text size: Default, Large (125%), Extra large (150%), and Largest (200%).
  • Theme: Default (warm off-white paper), Dark (warm dark brown), and High contrast (black background with white text and yellow links).
  • Your preferences are stored only in your browser’s local storage and are applied automatically on future visits.
  • If your operating system has “Increase contrast” or “Dark mode” enabled, the site automatically adopts the matching theme.

What We Have Done

  • Standard semantic HTML structure: proper heading hierarchy and navigation, main, and footer landmarks for screen reader navigation.
  • A “Skip to main content” link at the very beginning of every page.
  • Full keyboard operability with clearly visible focus indicators.
  • A target text-to-background contrast ratio of 7:1 (WCAG AAA) across all three themes, verified by automated checks.
  • All body links are underlined; the current page in navigation is indicated by both shape and bold text rather than color alone.
  • Links that open in a new tab are explicitly labeled in their link text.
  • No auto-playing audio or video, flashing content, or time limits. The header logo plays a brief ~4-second ripple animation once on load and stops; page transitions animate the active navigation pill and fade content over ~0.4 seconds. All animations are disabled when “Reduce motion” is enabled in your system settings.
  • Relative units throughout, so layouts reflow cleanly when text size is enlarged.
  • Decorative graphics (the home hero artwork, footer lockup, and action page badge) are hidden from assistive technologies.
  • Buttons, navigation links, language switcher links, and reading controls have touch targets of at least about 44 × 44 px; inline text links and article category tags are smaller.
  • Wide tables inside articles can be focused with the keyboard and scrolled horizontally.

Known Limitations

  • This website has not undergone a third-party accessibility audit.
  • Headings use serif fonts, but because we do not load external web fonts, appearance varies slightly depending on the fonts installed on your device.
  • This site contains no photographs, video, or audio. Informational graphics are limited to the role selection illustration on the About & Joining page and diagrams in certain articles; each includes alternative text, a caption, and equivalent information in the surrounding text. If we add images, video, or audio in the future, we will include alt text, captions, or transcripts.

Contact Us

For media inquiries, organizational collaboration, corrections, or accessibility feedback, please email:

info@asdtw.org

When reporting an accessibility issue, please let us know which page you were on and what device, browser, and assistive technology you use so we can address it promptly.

Privacy Notice

  • This site uses no cookies, no analytics or tracking tools, and loads no external fonts or external scripts.
  • Reading settings are stored solely in your browser’s local storage and are never transmitted.
  • We do not publish photographs or member rosters.
  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log connection metadata under its own policies.
  • Following the Discord invite link takes you to Discord, where Discord’s privacy policy applies.
  • If you subscribe to the newsletter, the email address you enter is sent to and stored by Buttondown to deliver it, and Buttondown’s privacy policy applies. Every issue includes an unsubscribe link.
